# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, appearance, disability, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or technology preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and confidentiality.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.
- Prioritize community safety over winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify standards, remove or edit contributions, issue warnings, temporarily or permanently restrict participation, and report credible threats. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, consistent, and mindful of affected people's safety.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the hosting platform until a dedicated conduct address is published. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible; retaliation is prohibited.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant; consider adopting its current full text if a larger contributor community forms.
